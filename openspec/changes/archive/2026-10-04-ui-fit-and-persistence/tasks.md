## 1. Camera-following map viewport (defect 1)

- [ ] 1.1 Add a pure camera-offset helper (e.g. `src/ui/logic/camera.ts`) exporting an offset function of `(mapAxisPx, viewportAxisPx, playerAxisTiles, tileSize)` returning a clamped, tile-aligned integer offset. The offset formula and its single canonical range `[min(0, viewportAxis − mapAxis), 0]` are defined in design D1 — reference D1 rather than re-transcribing them. The lower bound must be `min(0, viewportAxis − mapAxis)` (NOT `min(0, mapAxis − viewportAxis)`, which inverts to a positive bound for a map larger than the viewport and pins the camera at 0). Centering uses the player tile's top-left origin `playerAxis * TILE_SIZE`; both `TILE_SIZE` and the measured axes are dp, so offsets are exact integers. Verify a new `src/ui/logic/camera.test.ts` covers centering, left/top-edge clamp, right/bottom-edge clamp, fit-to-viewport `0` (range `[0,0]`), and unmeasured `0`, asserting exact values (no tolerance)
